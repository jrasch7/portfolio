# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, no framework. Decided earlier in this project: no external CDNs or webfont hosts (strict CSP `default-src 'self'`), so any typeface must be self-hosted as a file under `assets/`. Published on GitHub Pages.

## Users

Confirmed: companies that hire. Recruiters and tech leads deciding whether to talk to João Vargas for a job or contract. They skim fast, then look for proof of depth: how he structures systems, what he verifies, how he works with AI agents. Success is that they leave wanting to talk: e-mail or LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio for João Vargas, full-stack engineer in Novo Hamburgo (RS, Brazil). It exists to show technical judgment, not a list of projects: the architecture of Nivela, how tenant isolation and the AI confirmation flow work, and a real CI/CD pipeline whose live status is shown on the page itself.

## Positioning

A portfolio that proves its claims by being them: the demos run in the page, the numbers are counted from real code, and the pipeline report on the page is generated by the pipeline that built the page. A neighbour could not copy that without having the same engineering underneath.

## Operating Context

- Content lives in `index.html` (PT) plus `assets/i18n.js` (EN) and `assets/app.js` (demos, projects, pipeline screen).
- Bilingual PT/EN with a toggle; PT is the default for Brazilian visitors.
- Real screenshots only (public Nivela demo, SulCore demo); nothing from a product that is not in production. Nivela Pay is explicitly excluded.
- The page ships with a pipeline: `npm run pipeline`, tests in `tests/`, report in `pipeline.json`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must keep working: language toggle, three interactive demos (architecture, tenant isolation, Nivela AI), gallery with lightbox, command palette (Ctrl K), pipeline screen, projects, stack, contact (e-mail, LinkedIn, GitHub).
- Hard constraints: no third-party requests, no inline styles or scripts (CSP), images in WebP within budgets (<=150 KB each), Lighthouse >= 90/95/95/90, axe with no serious or critical violations, no emoji in the UI, strict public-repo privacy (no IPs, no customer data).
- Facts that must stay true: counts (290+ services, 560+ routes, 200+ migrations, 390+ test files) are measured on the Nivela codebase and rounded down.
- Open: the owner's personal photo is not available; the About section shows a placeholder until `assets/foto.jpg` exists.

## Brand Commitments

Name: João Vargas. Voice: direct, concrete, first person, no marketing adjectives. Language: Brazilian Portuguese first, English equivalent. The previous dark-purple SaaS look was rejected by the owner as "generic"; he asked for a unique, distinctive identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public Nivela demo captures in `assets/img/` (dashboard, mobile).
- SulCore demo capture.
- Measured pipeline results in `pipeline.json` (local run: Lighthouse 100/100/100/100).
